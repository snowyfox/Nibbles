Keypad: simulated
Keypad LOCKED: hold UNLOCK (U) for 1 s

Keys: 1-8 numbers · A all · B body · E eyes · F fins · P page · Shift or L LATCH · Space TAKE · X flash · Z blackout · S fin sensor · U unlock (hold 1 s).
(Letters go by what the key types, so any layout works, Dvorak included.)
Number pad: 1-8 numbers · 0 TAKE · Enter LATCH · / body · * eyes · − all · = (or Clear) fins · + page · . flash · 9 blackout.
Everything is a bump until it's latched: hold a number to show that preset on the target, and tap LATCH while holding it to keep it (the old Live drops into Standby). Hold TAKE to show Standby; tap LATCH while holding it to keep the swap. LATCH held first, then a number, cues that preset in Standby unseen; LATCH + TAKE copies Live back to Standby. PAGE + number sets brightness. The STANDBY panel works as TAKE by touch.
The body's commands go to the WLED card's address over its JSON API (bumps are imitated: on the shark they go by radio); the eyes' go to the simulated eyes.
